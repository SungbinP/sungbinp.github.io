Welcome to my webpage!

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at the [Schar School of Policy and Government](https://schar.gmu.edu/), [George Mason University](https://www.gmu.edu/).

My research interests are in labor economics, health economics, and applied microeconomics. My [job market paper](https://sungbinp.github.io/assets/pdf/Park_JMP_UI_Medicaid_Notch.pdf) examines how Medicaid eligibility rules affect workers' decisions to claim unemployment insurance by creating trade-offs between health insurance coverage and UI benefits.

I am on the 2026–2027 job market.
